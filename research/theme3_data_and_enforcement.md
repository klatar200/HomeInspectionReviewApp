# Theme 3: Large-scale complaint data and enforcement vs. production homebuilders

AS-OF: 2026-10-14 (research date). Read-only web research; nothing in the git repo was edited.

## How to read this file
- "Fetched" = I opened the page and extracted the figure. "Search-result only" = figure appeared in a search-result summary but I did not open the underlying page (verify before relying on it).
- Fetch tool summarizes pages with a small model; numbers were taken from those extractions. Re-verify any figure before it goes into product copy.
- Allegations are labelled "alleged". Only court or regulator findings are stated as findings. Most settlements below had no admission of liability.
- Blocked pages (403/401/404/503): jdpower.com press page, builderonline.com articles (several), probuilder.com, azag.gov, nationalmortgageprofessional.com (AZ AG/Pulte), consumeraffairs.com (FL AG/Pulte, FTC/KB 2005), moneywise.com, inman.com, fbi.gov, justice.gov (2 pages), Taylor Morrison law-firm page (503). Facts from those are search-result only.

## Top-line caveats
1. There is NO current public J.D. Power US New-Home Builder study. J.D. Power announced on 2011-07-21 it would not publish the 2011 edition; the last edition found is 2010. I found no evidence of a revival (searches 2024-2026 returned nothing). Do not cite J.D. Power as current.
2. The only current large-scale satisfaction dataset is Eliant (now owned by Zonda): 210,000+ surveys, 250+ builders, but only awards/winners are public; defect rates and national benchmarks are not public. Builders' own Eliant claims are marketing.
3. No FTC, CFPB, or state-AG action against D.R. Horton, Lennar, Taylor Morrison, M/I, Meritage, Century, or NVR over defects or sales practices was found. Enforcement hits found are old (KB Home FTC 2005, KB Home FL 2016, Pulte FL 2018-19, Pulte AZ 2010) or concern the mortgage affiliate (Lennar/Eagle FHA FCA 2018, Beazer 2009).
4. The 2025-26 pressure is private litigation and journalism (Hunterbrook, WSJ), not regulators. The current CFPB has been dismissing pre-existing actions (per law-firm blog on Colony Ridge).
5. No documented enforcement or lawsuit was found on "bait-and-switch lot premiums", price increases after contract, or closing delays at production builders. This is a gap, not evidence of absence.

Findings and limits:
- BBB profiles are fragmented by division and market; one search for "D.R. Horton" returned 14 profiles, "Pulte Homes" 15 (mostly TX), "KB Home" scattered. A cross-builder comparison on BBB counts is unreliable. Counts are not normalized by homes closed (D.R. Horton closed 84,863 homes in FY2025).
- Complaint-TYPE breakdowns (Product/Service, Billing, Guarantee/Warranty, Sales Practices) were NOT visible in the page text I could fetch (the "complaints" subpages returned only totals). Scripted/JS rendering likely hides them. Nothing here supports a type breakdown.
- A+ ratings coexist with hundreds of complaints. Do not equate BBB grade with quality.
- Other sites: ComplaintsBoard lists D.R. Horton 225 complaints and Lennar 208 complaints (unverified user-posted, search-result only). Trustpilot Lennar reportedly 1.8 (search-result only). Treat as anecdote.
- Eliant public reviews exist per builder (e.g., Century Complete Triangle NC: 94 reviews Nov 2023-May 2025, comments about sales/closing like late HOA disclosure) but are builder-curated: https://reviews.eliant.com/century-complete/flagmarsh (search-result only).
